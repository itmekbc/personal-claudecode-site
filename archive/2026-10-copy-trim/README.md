Snapshot of site copy before the case study trim and the brand-first
repositioning. Files mirror their original paths in the repo, so any of
them can be diffed against or copied back over the live version.

Nothing here is loaded by the site: `caseStudyLoader.js` only globs
`src/case-studies/*/`.

- `src/case-studies/*/case-study.json` — long-form case study copy
  (overview, challenge, approach, user experience, solution) for the AI
  Learning App, Agility Website, Tradeshow Experience, Robot Livery, and
  Hero Brand Film, plus the TIME, Rebrand, and Studio Shoot entries as they
  were.
- `src/data/resume.json`, `index.html`, `src/components/About.jsx`,
  `src/components/Portfolio.jsx`, `src/hooks/usePageMeta.js` — the
  product-first tagline, bio, About copy, page meta, and tag order.
